Hello, little one… come close. Are you cozy? … Good. Because tonight I'm going to tell you about the bravest kid in the whole Bible… a shepherd boy named David.

David was the youngest in his family. He had seven big brothers — seven! — and everybody thought of him as "the little one." His job was taking care of the sheep. He fed them… he found them cool water… and at night, under all the stars, he would play his harp and sing songs to God. The sheep loved his songs. … I did too.

Now, in those days there was trouble. A great big army came to fight God's people. And that army had a secret weapon… a giant named Goliath.

And when I say giant… I mean GIANT. Goliath was taller than your door. Taller than your dad standing on your mom's shoulders! His armor was shiny and heavy, and his spear was as big as a flagpole. And every single morning, he would stomp out — boom… boom… boom — and shout in his biggest, rumbliest voice: "Who will come and fight ME?"

And you know what all the soldiers did? All the big, strong soldiers with their swords and shields? … They hid. Every morning, for forty whole days. They were just so scared.

One day, David's daddy said, "David, please take some bread and cheese to your brothers at the army camp." So David went. And just as he got there… BOOM, boom, boom… out stomped Goliath, shouting his big shout. And all the soldiers ran and hid again.

But David didn't run. David stood very still… and he said something amazing. He said, "Why is everyone afraid? That giant is big… but our God is SO much bigger!"

The king heard about this brave boy and called for him. "You're just a kid," the king said. But David smiled and said, "When a lion tried to take one of my sheep, God helped me chase it away. When a bear came, God helped me again. God always helps me… and He'll help me now."

The king tried to give David his own royal armor — a big helmet that slid down over David's eyes, and a heavy coat that went clunk, clunk when he walked. David could barely move! "No thank you," he said. "I'll just go as me." … And that's a secret for you, little one: God doesn't need you to be anyone else. He made you YOU on purpose.

So David walked down to a little stream, and he picked out five smooth stones… plink, plink, plink, plink, plink… right into his shepherd's bag. He held his sling — that's like a little leather strap that can toss a stone really far and really fast. And he walked right out to meet the giant.

Goliath looked down… and down… and down… and laughed his meanest laugh. "A little boy? HA! Go home, little boy!"

But David wasn't shaking. Not even his knees. He called up to that giant: "You come with a sword and a spear… but I come in the name of the Lord! And today, everyone is going to see that my God is real!"

Then David ran — yes, RAN, right toward the giant! He put one smooth stone in his sling… he swung it around and around and around… whoosh, whoosh, whoosh… and he let it go!

The stone flew through the air like a shooting star… and bonk! It hit that giant right in the forehead. And do you know what happened? Goliath wobbled… and he swayed… and then — BOOM! — down he fell, flat on the ground, and he did not get up. The fight was over, just like that!

And all the soldiers who had been hiding jumped up and cheered — "Hooray for David! Hooray for the Lord!" The scary giant was beaten… by a little shepherd boy… and one smooth stone… and one great big God.

Now snuggle down, little one, and remember this as you close your eyes… Sometimes things in this world look awfully big. Big worries… big fears… maybe even a big, dark bedroom at night. But none of them — not one — is bigger than Me. And I am always, always with you… just like I was with David.

So be brave, little shepherd… I love you so much. … Goodnight.
